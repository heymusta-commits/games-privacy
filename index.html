Privacy policy

How Oasis handles game progress, advertising, and the information you send us.

At a glance

Your game progress stays on your device. Advertising is provided through Google AdMob, and support through email. The current version is free and has no in-app purchases.

Who is responsible

This policy applies to the iPhone game Oasis Energy Links, including its Tower Rescue mode (together, “Oasis” or “the game”). The developer responsible for the game is Fatima Zahra Elkemmal, Morocco. For privacy questions or player support, email shopyleads@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

Oasis does not require you to create a game account. Progress, unlocked levels, scores, tutorial status, and sound and motion preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not operate a developer-hosted cloud save or multiplayer service.

Local game data is used to resume your game and remember your preferences. It may be included in device backups managed by your operating system. Deleting the app and its local data removes the local save; a backup restore may restore saved data.

Advertising and privacy choices

Oasis integrates Google AdMob to provide banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Development builds may show Google test advertisements.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process network information, including your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic and performance information. This supports ad delivery, measurement, reliability, and fraud prevention. Available information depends on device settings, permissions, region, and advertising configuration.

The current game requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising can still involve device information and identifiers for functions such as measurement and fraud prevention; it does not mean that no data is processed.

Where required, the game asks for advertising privacy choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. When available, use the advertising privacy options in the game’s Settings to review or change those choices. You can also manage relevant privacy permissions in iPhone Settings. Changing a choice does not affect processing that already occurred lawfully.

Rewarded videos for hints are optional. The current version does not offer a paid ad-removal option.

Learn more from Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or details you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is handled using Gmail. We keep it only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. You can ask us to delete support information, subject to those obligations.

Service providers and retention

Google processes advertising information as described above. Apple may process information independently when distributing the game or providing operating-system services, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and prevent abuse. We do not run a separate game-account database or a separate gameplay analytics service in the current version.

Google, Apple, and email service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their privacy policies describe their handling, retention, and applicable safeguards. The developer cannot delete records held independently by Apple or Google; requests concerning those records should also be directed to the relevant provider.

Local game data remains on your device until removed or replaced. Support retention is described above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, request a copy, or withdraw consent. Contact shopyleads@gmail.com with the subject “Oasis privacy request.” We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify the request, and respond as required by applicable law.

We process support information to respond to your request and maintain the service. Where applicable, advertising processing relies on the consent collected through the privacy message or another basis available under applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

You can remove local game data through your device’s app deletion controls.

Children and family use

We do not ask players to submit their name, age, email address, or other personal details to play. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to our support inbox can contact us to request review and deletion, subject to applicable obligations. Advertising services may process device information as explained above.

This website and policy updates

This privacy and support website does not include advertising, forms, or analytics scripts added by the developer. Its hosting provider may process standard request and security logs, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. Selecting an email link opens your email application; no support message is sent automatically.

We will update this page when the game’s features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
