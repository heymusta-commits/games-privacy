Support for Oasis Energy Links and Tower Rescue on iPhone.

Contact the developer

Email us with your game mode, level, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are welcome if they do not contain private information.

Email support

shopyleads@gmail.com · Opens your email app. You review and send the message yourself.

Advertising and hints

The game is free and supported by advertising. Rewarded videos for hints are optional. If a video is unavailable, you can keep playing and try again later.

Missing progress

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app or moving to a different device can affect your save. Oasis does not currently provide a developer-hosted cloud save.

Privacy requests

Email the same support address with the subject “Oasis privacy request.” Read the privacy policy for information about advertising and local saves.
